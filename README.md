# Flametree

## Omnichannel AI Agents for Customer Operations

Controlled AI customer interactions with full customer context and complete visibility.

Flametree is an AI customer operations platform for banks and fintechs. It helps teams deploy AI agents across customer acquisition, onboarding, service operations, and collections.

## Use Cases

- **Acquisition & Conversion** — engage visitors and convert them into qualified leads.
- **Onboarding & Activation** — identify why users drop off and guide them to completion.
- **Service Operations** — resolve customer requests, carry out operations, and hand off to a human when needed.
- **Collections Management** — manage timely, two-way customer engagement and repayment workflows.

## Platform

- Omnichannel AI agents with customer context and interaction history
- Controlled agent behavior and AI-human handoff with Copilot capabilities
- Outbound campaigns and analytics across agents and customer journeys
- Model-agnostic architecture with cloud, private cloud, and on-premises deployment
- Communication across web and mobile apps, messengers, email, SMS, and voice
- Integrations with back-end systems through APIs and standard protocols
